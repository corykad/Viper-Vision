Basic Controls

Use Start, Pause, Stop, Return to dock, Locate, and Spot clean from the Vacuum tab.

Suction And Mop Settings

Viper discovers Roborock select, number, and child lock entities from Home Assistant. Only controls Home Assistant exposes can be shown.

Room Cleaning

  1. Choose your vacuum entity.
  2. Press Refresh room list.
  3. Use arrow keys to move through rooms.
  4. Press Space to check or uncheck rooms.
  5. Choose repeat count.
  6. Press Clean selected rooms.
