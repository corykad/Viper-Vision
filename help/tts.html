What TTS Does

TTS is the voice Viper uses for doorbell descriptions, utility alerts, and manual broadcasts.

Recommended Beginner Settings

  • Use Gemini if you like the Gemini voices and accept cloud latency.
  • Use Edge TTS if you want a fast free cloud voice.
  • Keep doorbell speed fast.
  • Keep utilities and broadcasts normal or brisk.
  • Leave Gemini warmup off unless you accept possible API charges.

Per-Alert Categories

  • Doorbell: short, urgent, fast speech.
  • Utilities: fridge, freezer, battery, filter, and status messages.
  • Manual broadcast: text you type and send to speakers.

Dynamic Mood

Dynamic Mood lets Viper adjust speech style from the message. Urgent doorbell alerts can sound faster and more alert. Friendly messages can sound warmer. Utility warnings can sound calmer or firmer.

Gemini Warmup And Cost

Gemini warmup sends requests to Gemini to keep the connection ready. Those requests may be billed. Keep warmup off unless you choose lower first-alert delay over possible cost.

If Speech Is Too Slow

  1. Open Diagnostics.
  2. Run speed diagnostics.
  3. Compare camera capture, Gemini vision, TTS generation, and speaker playback.
  4. If Gemini TTS is the slow part, use Edge TTS for doorbells and keep Gemini for utilities or broadcasts.
