Ring Doorbells And Live Video

Back to help index

The Simple Explanation

Viper needs two things for each Ring doorbell you want to use.

  1. A trigger entity, so Viper knows the doorbell rang or motion happened.
  2. A live RTSP stream, so Viper can capture a current image for Gemini.

Two Ring Logins

This is the part that trips people up. There are two different Ring sign-ins.

  • Home Assistant Ring integration: gives Viper trigger entities like ding, motion, or person detected.
  • Ring-MQTT: gives Viper live RTSP video streams.

Signing in to one does not sign in to the other. For the full Viper doorbell flow, most Ring users need both.

Step 1: Sign In To Ring In Home Assistant

  1. Open Home Assistant in your browser.
  2. Go to Settings, Devices and services.
  3. Add the Ring integration, or open it if it is already installed.
  4. Sign in with your Ring account.
  5. Return to Viper and let it discover devices again.
  6. On the Confirm Doorbell Triggers step, choose the front and back doorbell trigger entities.

Step 2: Install Mosquitto And Ring-MQTT

Use Viper's Ring-MQTT Live Video step. Viper can install Mosquitto and Ring-MQTT with Video Streaming on Home Assistant OS when the Home Assistant token has permission.

If Viper cannot install them automatically, follow Mosquitto And Ring-MQTT Step By Step.

Step 3: Sign In To Ring-MQTT

  1. Let Viper open the Ring-MQTT app page in your normal browser.
  2. If Home Assistant asks for login, sign in to Home Assistant first.
  3. On the Ring-MQTT app page, tab to Open Web UI and activate it.
  4. Sign in to Ring inside Ring-MQTT.
  5. Return to Viper after Ring-MQTT shows your Ring devices.

Step 4: Find And Test Streams

  1. In Viper, press Find And Test Doorbell Cameras.
  2. Viper checks Ring-MQTT logs, Home Assistant attributes, and MQTT topics.
  3. Viper does not guess RTSP URLs from Home Assistant camera snapshot names.
  4. Choose the stream for each door you use.
  5. Press Save Selected Camera Streams.
  6. Press the camera test button for each configured door.

If You Have One Doorbell

Configure only that door. Leave the other door disabled or unassigned. Viper does not require a back door if you do not use one.

If You Have More Than Two Ring Cameras

Viper can discover more than two streams. Choose which stream belongs to the front door and which belongs to the back door. Ignore cameras you do not want Viper to use for doorbell alerts.

RTSP URL Pattern

Ring-MQTT streams usually look like this:

rtsp://YOUR_HOME_ASSISTANT_IP:8554/CAMERA_ID_live

You normally should not type this by hand. Let Viper find and test the streams from Ring-MQTT.

Common Problems

  • No trigger entities: fix the Home Assistant Ring integration first.
  • No RTSP streams: fix Ring-MQTT login or video streaming first.
  • Camera test says unavailable: confirm Ring-MQTT is running and port 8554 is exposed.
  • Wrong door speaks: change the trigger or stream combo box in the wizard and save again.
